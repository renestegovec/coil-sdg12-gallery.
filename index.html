The problem beneath the surface.

OUR CHALLENGE

Our oceans need protection.

Marine ecosystems face serious threats from plastic pollution, overfishing, habitat destruction and rising ocean temperatures. Plastic waste can injure marine animals, while damaged habitats make it harder for underwater species to survive.

Protecting the ocean requires action from governments, businesses and individuals. Reducing single-use plastics, choosing sustainable seafood and keeping beaches clean are practical ways to help.

Research local examples of marine pollution and add your own findings. Support factual claims with reliable sources.
