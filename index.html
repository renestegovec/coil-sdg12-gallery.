Why does the ocean matter?

SDG 14 is one of the United Nations' goals. Its mission is simple: protect life under water.

The ocean gives us food, helps control the weather and is home to amazing creatures. But pollution, plastic and climate change are putting it in danger.

Even small actions can make a big difference. We all have a part to play in protecting our blue planet.

Make a difference

What is hurting our ocean?

Our ocean faces many problems, but we can help by understanding them and changing our habits.

🧴 Plastic Pollution

Plastic bags and bottles can end up in the sea. Sea animals may eat them or get trapped in them.

🐠 Animals in Danger

Many sea animals are losing their homes. Too much fishing and dirty water make life harder for them.

🌡️ A Warming Ocean

The ocean is getting warmer. This can harm coral reefs and make it harder for some sea animals to survive.

How can YOU help?

You don't need to do something huge. Simple daily choices can help protect the ocean.

♻️

Use Less Plastic

Bring a reusable water bottle and shopping bag. Avoid single-use plastic whenever possible.

🏖️

Keep Beaches Clean

Never leave rubbish on the beach. Pick up litter when it is safe to do so.

🐬

Spread the Word

Tell your friends why the ocean matters. Share ideas and inspire others to take action.
